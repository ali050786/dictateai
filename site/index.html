Questions.

Is Utter free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT License, with no account, subscription or word limit. If you use the optional cloud cleanup, you bring your own OpenRouter key and pay OpenRouter directly.

How is it different from macOS Dictation?

macOS Dictation types what you said, word for word. Utter also drops fillers, follows corrections, adds punctuation and learns how you spell names. You can use any key, it keeps a history, and there's no time limit.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download the models the first time: about 160 MB for speech (646 MB for languages Phonon doesn't cover) and, if you turn on AI cleanup, 1.7 to 4.3 GB. After that it works offline.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Utter is signed ad hoc and not notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer account. macOS asks you to confirm it once in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The source is on GitHub if you'd like to check it.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To notice the dictation key in any app and to put the text at your cursor. It also reads that text field right after a dictation, to learn from fixes you make. None of it leaves your Mac.

Which languages work?

English and 24 European languages with Phonon, and Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Chinese, Korean and others with Whisper. Auto-detect covers Phonon's languages. For the others, choose your language in Settings.

Will it work on my Intel Mac?

No. The speech and cleanup models run on Apple's MLX and Core ML, which need an M-series chip.

What does the cloud option send?

Only the text of your dictation, to the OpenRouter model you pick, for cleanup. Your audio and the speech recognition stay on your Mac. Without a key, nothing leaves it.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar and move Utter to the Trash. To remove its models, settings and history too, delete the .dictateai folder in your home folder.
