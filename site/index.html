How it works

One key. Three moves.

DictateAI sits in your menu bar. There's no window to switch to and nothing to paste. The default key is Right ⌥ Option, and you can change it to any key you like.

hold

Hold to talk

Hold the key and speak. Let go, and the text appears where your cursor is.

⌥⌥

Double-tap for hands-free

Tap twice to keep listening without holding anything. Tap once more to stop.

⌥⌥⌥

Triple-tap to undo

Didn't come out right? Triple-tap and your last dictation is removed.

Features

Everything you need to stop typing.

Built for people who write all day: emails, messages, prompts, docs and code comments.

Types into any app

Text goes in at your cursor in native apps, browsers and Electron apps. If an app won't accept typed text, it pastes and then puts your clipboard back, images included.

AI cleanup on your Mac

A local model (Qwen 2.5 on Apple's MLX) removes filler words, fixes punctuation and handles "no wait, I mean…". Turn it on in Settings whenever you like.

No word limits

Everything runs on your hardware, so there's no monthly cap and no usage meter. Dictate as much as you like.

26 languages and auto-detect

Speech recognition with Whisper (via WhisperKit). Pick a language or let it detect yours. Hindi stays in Devanagari, Urdu in its own script: cleanup never switches your writing system.

A dictionary that learns

Add names, product terms and jargon yourself, or let DictateAI learn when you fix the same word twice.

History

Your last 100 dictations are kept on your Mac. Each is tagged Local or Cloud so you know where it was cleaned. Clear them anytime.

Your shortcut

Right ⌥ Option by default. Change the dictation key in Settings to whatever fits your hands.

A small, quiet pill

A slim indicator shows when it's listening and transcribing. Put it in any of six spots along the top or bottom of your screen.

Mutes while you speak

Optionally mutes your Mac's audio while you dictate, then restores it. If you'd already muted it, it stays muted.

Pick your microphone

Use the built-in mic, AirPods or a USB mic. Short one- or two-word replies are picked up too.

Optional cloud, your key

Prefer a bigger model? Add your own OpenRouter key and pick any model. The key is encrypted in your macOS Keychain, and if the cloud is busy, your Mac takes over.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under the MIT licence. Read it, build it yourself or improve it.

Privacy

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Most dictation apps send your audio to a server. DictateAI does it all locally, unless you add your own cloud key.

  • No account, sign-up or tracking
  • History, dictionary and settings are stored in ~/.dictateai, readable only by you
  • Logs record how long a dictation was, not what you said
  • Dictated text is hidden from clipboard managers
  • Cloud cleanup is off until you add your own key

Where it's available

Made for Apple Silicon Macs.

Download and drag to Applications

Open the .dmg and drag DictateAI into your Applications folder. That's the whole install: the speech and AI engines are built in, so there's nothing to set up in Terminal.

Open it the first time with right-click

In Applications, right-click DictateAI and choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

Why? DictateAI is free and isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. You can read every line of the code on GitHub.

Allow Accessibility

When macOS asks, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and switch on DictateAI. This lets it hear your dictation key and type into other apps. The key starts working right away.

Allow the microphone and start talking

Hold Right ⌥ Option and speak. The first time, DictateAI fetches its speech model, which takes about a minute. After that it works offline. DictateAI lives in the menu bar 🎙, not the Dock.

Turn on AI cleanup (optional)

Click 🎙 in the menu bar → Open DictateAI → Settings → AI Text Cleanup. The model downloads once in the background, and you can keep dictating plain text while it does.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is DictateAI really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence, with no subscription, trial or word limit. The only possible cost is the optional cloud cleanup, which you pay to OpenRouter directly with your own key.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download its models the first time. After that, dictation and cleanup work offline.

How much space does it use?

The app is 274 MB, and the speech model it fetches on first launch is about 500 MB. AI cleanup is optional; turning it on adds a model of 1.7 GB for English, or 4.3 GB for other languages, since a larger model is needed to keep Hindi, Arabic and others in their own script.

How is this different from macOS Dictation?

DictateAI adds an AI cleanup step: it removes "um" and "uh", fixes punctuation, handles corrections like "no wait, Thursday", and applies your personal dictionary. It works with a single push-to-talk key in any app and has no time limit.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, which needs a paid developer account. Right-click → Open the first time and macOS will remember your choice.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To notice when you press the dictation key while another app is in front, and to place text at your cursor. It doesn't read your screen, and it doesn't record anything unless you're holding the key or in hands-free mode.

Which languages work?

26, including English, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese and more, plus auto-detect. English uses a faster 3B cleanup model and other languages a 7B model. You can choose a faster or more accurate model in Settings.

Will it work on my Intel Mac?

No. The local models rely on Apple Silicon (M1 or later).

What happens if I use the cloud option?

Only the transcribed text, never your audio, is sent to the OpenRouter model you choose, using your own key. If the cloud is rate-limited or fails, your Mac cleans the text instead, and you'll get a quiet notice.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar, delete DictateAI from Applications, and delete the ~/.dictateai folder to remove your history, dictionary and speech model. The cleanup models are in ~/.cache/huggingface.
